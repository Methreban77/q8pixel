The dates in PRE were turned away for their looks

Kuwait grows over 100,000 tonnes of dates a year. Some are too small, split or marked to sell as fruit, but they are perfectly good to eat. We buy them, blend them, and tell you exactly how much.

  • Made in Kuwait

    Mixed, pressed and packed by a licensed food maker in Kuwait. The dates are Kuwaiti; puffed rice, whey, coffee and cocoa are imported, so we never say “100% Kuwaiti”.

Questions

Can I eat both bars at once?

Yes. Together they are a balanced snack of about 100 g with carbohydrate and protein. The split is a convenience, not a rule.

Is 3 g of creatine a day safe?

The International Society of Sports Nutrition calls 3–5 g a day effective and finds no compelling evidence of harm in healthy adults. If you have a medical condition, ask your doctor first.

Does creatine work better after training?

We don't claim that. Taking it every day matters more than the hour. It sits in POST because that is when most people already have their protein.

Why a paper wrapper?

One wrapper with two sealed compartments uses less material than two separately wrapped bars, and needs no box.

I have an allergy. Can I eat DUOFUEL?

Not yet: both packs contain milk and almonds, and PRE contains caffeine; the label says so. Gluten-free, lactose-free and nut-free versions are planned for year 2–3.

Where is DUOFUEL made?

In Kuwait, by a licensed food manufacturer working to our recipe. The dates come from Kuwaiti farms; the puffed rice, whey protein, coffee and cocoa are imported.

Is there caffeine?

Yes, from coffee in the PRE bar: about 60 mg in Gym and about 35 mg in Life, less than a typical cup of coffee. It is declared on the label; not for children or during pregnancy. POST has no coffee.

Are surplus dates lower quality?

Only in looks. They pass the same food-safety tests as any date we use; they were just too small or marked for the fruit shelf.
