Our story

Born between the harbour and the coals

Marsa Ember began with a simple ritual: be at the harbour when the boats come in, choose the best of the catch, and cook it the way Kuwaiti families always have — over real charcoal, with patience.

Our kitchen keeps the old flavours — loomi, saffron, bezar and cardamom — and serves them in a warm, modern room by the sea, made for long family dinners and late evenings with friends.

  • Morning catch

    Hamour, zubaidi and sobaity chosen fresh every day.

  • Natural charcoal

    Slow heat and real smoke — no gas, no shortcuts.

  • Gulf spices

    Our own bezar blend, ground in the kitchen every week.
